## Our story

**H2:** A design company built for how India lives now.

**Body:**
Interior design in India has long been split between two worlds. On one side, talented boutique studios — deeply personal, but often slow, opaque about cost, and hard to scale. On the other, large platforms — fast and predictable, but templated, impersonal, and quick to cut corners.

Luxe Axis was founded on a simple conviction: you shouldn't have to choose. Technology, used with restraint and taste, can give you the best of both — the soul of a boutique studio with the transparency, speed, and reliability of a company engineered for 2026.

We are Chennai-born and Chennai-proud. We design for how South India actually lives — for joint families and festivals, for Chennai's heat and humidity, for Vastu that deserves to be respected rather than dismissed. And we back every project with something the industry rarely offers: a written promise.

## Our values

**H2:** The five principles behind every decision.

**Spacefulness**
Every design decision respects and maximises the human experience of space. Not just how a room looks — how it feels to live in, move through, and come home to.

Every project starts from eco-conscious material choices. Sustainability is our default setting, not a premium add-on.

AI assists; humans decide and create. Our technology exists to free our designers to do their most human, most valuable work — understanding you.

## The journey overview

**H2:** From first hello to handover, here's exactly what happens.

**Intro:** Every Luxe Axis project follows a clear, structured path. You always know what stage you're in, what's next, and what it costs. Here's how it unfolds.

---

## Stage 1 — Discover

**Eyebrow:** Understanding you

**H3:** We learn your space, your life, and your budget.

**Body:** It starts with a conversation — no obligation, no pressure. We talk through what you want, how you live, your timeline, and a realistic budget. For projects that move forward, we run a structured Design Brief Deep-Dive: measurements, lifestyle, must-haves, Vastu preferences, and inspiration. Everything is captured in your Space OS project file, so nothing gets lost.

**You get:** A clear brief, an honest view of what's achievable within your budget, and a recommended service tier.

---

## Stage 2 — Design

**Eyebrow:** Intelligent concepts

**H3:** Ideas you can see, refined with you.

**Body:** This is where intelligence meets imagination. Our designers develop concepts accelerated by our AI tools — multiple layout options, Vastu-checked against your floor plan, and brought to life in photorealistic 3D. You review, react, and refine. We iterate until it's unmistakably yours.

**You get:** Mood boards, space plans, Vastu compliance insight, and 3D visualisations of your finished space — before a single decision is locked.

---

## Stage 3 — Cost & Confirm

**Eyebrow:** Radical transparency

**H3:** A fixed quote, clear milestones, zero surprises.

**Body:** Once the design is agreed, we give you a detailed, transparent quotation — with a clear scope, itemised where it matters, and milestone-based payments. No vague "extras," no moving numbers. If material prices move materially, we tell you and show you why. You confirm with confidence.

**You get:** A fixed quote, a milestone payment schedule (typically 40% advance / 30% on concept approval / 20% on execution start / 10% on handover), GST clearly shown, and a written timeline commitment.

---

## Stage 4 — Build

**Eyebrow:** Execution, tracked live

**H3:** A dedicated project manager. Verified vendors. Full visibility.

**Body:** Your project moves to execution under a dedicated Project Manager who is your single point of contact throughout. We work only with a verified vendor network, held to our quality standards. Every milestone, update, and photo is logged on Space OS — so whether you're across the city or across the world, you can see progress in real time. All vendor coordination runs through your PM, never through you.

**You get:** A named PM, a live project dashboard, milestone updates, and a communication window that fits your day (including WhatsApp).

---

## Stage 5 — Handover & Care

**Eyebrow:** Getting it right

**H3:** Snag-free handover, in writing.

**Body:** Before we hand over your keys, we run a rigorous quality check through our Six-Gate quality management system, and we close 100% of snags first. You receive a written warranty appropriate to your tier — from 30 days up to 6 months of full cover. And if you'd like us to keep looking after your space, our Concierge plans are there for the long term.

**You get:** A snag-free handover, a written warranty, care documentation, and the option of ongoing Concierge support.

---

## Timeline & guarantees band

**H2:** Timelines you can plan around.

**Body:** Every tier carries a timeline commitment — for example, a 45-day guarantee on Essential projects, 60-day on Signature, and milestone-based delivery on Elite and larger works. These assume timely approvals, payments, and site access from your side, and are protected against events genuinely outside anyone's control. If we miss a commitment that's on us, we make it right.
