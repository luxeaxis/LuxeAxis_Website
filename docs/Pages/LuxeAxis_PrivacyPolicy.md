## 4. Personal data we collect

We collect only the personal data reasonably necessary to provide our Services. The categories below describe what we may collect depending on how you interact with us.

**Identity and contact data** — name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, postal and project address, and, for NRI clients, country of residence.

**Project and property data** — property type and size, floor plans, site photographs and videos, 3D scans, design briefs, style preferences, budget range, family/lifestyle information you choose to share to inform the design, and Vastu preferences.

**Account and platform data** — login credentials, Space OS activity, in-app messages, milestone approvals, document-vault contents, and support tickets.

**Transaction and financial data** — quotations, invoices, milestone billing records, payment status, and GST details. **We do not store your full card, UPI, or bank credentials** — payments are processed by our payment gateway (see Section 8).

**Subscription data** — plan, billing cycle, renewal and cancellation history, and service-usage records for Home Concierge, Elite Concierge, and Business Concierge.

**Marketing and communication data** — enquiry-form submissions, newsletter subscription status, campaign responses, and communication preferences.

**Technical and usage data** — IP address, device and browser type, operating system, pages viewed, referring URLs, and interactions on the Website, collected via cookies and similar technologies (see our [Cookie Policy](/cookies)).

**Sensitive / special-category data** — we do not seek sensitive personal data unless strictly necessary. Where you voluntarily share information such as health or accessibility needs relevant to a design, we process it only for that purpose and with your consent.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children (see Section 11).

## 10. Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

- **Access** — obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities.
- **Correction and updating** — have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected or completed.
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless retention is required by law.
- **Withdraw consent** — withdraw previously given consent, as easily as it was given.
- **Grievance redressal** — raise a grievance with us regarding our processing (see Section 13).
- **Nominate** — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@luxeaxis.in** or through the grievance channel in Section 13. We will respond within the timelines prescribed under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. There is no fee for exercising your rights, except where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

**Your responsibilities.** Under the DPDP Act, you must provide accurate information and not impersonate another person or suppress material information when providing personal data or filing a grievance.
