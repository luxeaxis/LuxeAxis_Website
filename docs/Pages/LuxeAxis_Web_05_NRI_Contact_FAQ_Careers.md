## Getting started

**How do I start a project with Luxe Axis?**
It begins with a short, no-obligation consultation. Tell us about your space, your goals, and your budget. If it's a fit, we move into a structured design brief and recommend the right service tier for you.

**Do you offer a free consultation?**
Yes — the initial consultation to understand your project is no-obligation. Detailed design work begins once you decide to proceed.

**Where are you based, and where do you work?**
We're based in Chennai and serve clients across Tamil Nadu, and — through Luxe Axis Digital and our Elite NRI service — clients across India and abroad.

## Pricing & payment

**How much does interior design cost in Chennai?**
It depends on your home's size, scope, and finishes. As a guide, residential projects typically range from Rs. 3.5L for a compact home to Rs. 25L+ for larger homes and villas, with design fees starting from Rs. 50,000. The best way to know is a quick fee estimate for your specific space.

**Why is the design fee separate from the project cost?**
We separate the professional design work from the build (execution and materials) so you can see exactly what you're paying for. It's part of our commitment to radical transparency.

**What's the payment schedule?**
Payments are milestone-based — typically 40% advance, 30% on concept approval, 20% at execution start, and 10% on handover. You pay as your project progresses.

**Are there hidden costs?**
No. Every cost is visible on your Space OS dashboard. If material prices move materially (beyond 8%), we tell you and show you why before proceeding.

**Do you charge GST?**
Yes — 18% GST applies to design services and is always shown clearly on your invoice.

## Process & timelines

**How long will my project take?**
Each tier carries a timeline commitment — for example, 45 days for Essential, 60 days for Signature, and milestone-based delivery for Elite and larger projects. These assume timely approvals, payments, and site access.

**Can I see my design before committing?**
Yes. You'll receive 3D visualisations of your space before decisions are locked — and with Elite, immersive walkthroughs. See it, refine it, then confirm.

**How do I track progress?**
Everything lives on Space OS — your live project dashboard, accessible anytime, from anywhere. You'll also have a dedicated project manager as your point of contact.

## Vastu & design

**Do you follow Vastu?**
Yes — and intelligently. Our proprietary Vastu-Tech AI reads your floor plan and scores its Vastu compliance, then suggests layouts that honour tradition without compromising modern living. Vastu is respected, never dismissed.

**Will my home look like a template?**
No. Personalisation is our core. Even at our Essential tier, we design around how *you* live — not a stock layout.

## NRI projects

**I live abroad — can you handle my Chennai project remotely?**
Absolutely. Our Elite tier is built for NRIs, with time-zone-friendly reviews, video walkthroughs, a live dashboard on Space OS, and a single point of contact. You'll always know exactly where your project stands.

**How do I make payments from abroad?**
Payments are milestone-based and handled securely. We'll guide you through the process, and everything is documented transparently on Space OS.

## After handover

**What happens at handover?**
We run a rigorous quality check and close 100% of snags *before* handover. You'll receive a written warranty and care documentation.

**What warranty do I get?**
It depends on your tier — from 30 days (Essential) to 90 days (Signature) to a 6-month full warranty (Elite). Snags reported soon after handover are covered at our cost.

**What if something needs attention later?**
Our Concierge plans provide ongoing, priority support beyond your warranty period — so your space stays cared for.
