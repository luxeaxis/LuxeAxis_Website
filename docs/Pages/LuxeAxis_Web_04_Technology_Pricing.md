## Hero
**Eyebrow:** Pricing
**H1:** Honest pricing, in plain sight.
**Subhead:** No hidden costs. No moving numbers. Just transparent, milestone-based pricing you can plan around — because trust starts with knowing exactly what you're paying for.

## How our pricing works band
**H2:** Radical transparency, by design.
**Body:** We separate our design fee (the professional work of designing your space) from execution and material costs (the build itself). You see both clearly. Payments are milestone-based — you pay as your project progresses, not all upfront. And every rupee is visible on your Space OS dashboard.

**Our pricing principles:**
- **Design fees are never discounted** — but scope can flex to fit your budget.
- **Milestone-based payments** — typically 40% advance / 30% on concept approval / 20% on execution start / 10% on handover.
- **GST shown clearly** — 18% on design services, always itemised.
- **Material cost protection** — if prices move materially (beyond 8%), we tell you and show you why.
- **Quotes with a clear validity** — 14 days (residential), 21 days (commercial), 30 days (NRI Elite).
