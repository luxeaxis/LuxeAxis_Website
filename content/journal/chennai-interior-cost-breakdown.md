# How to Estimate Your Chennai Home Interior Budget (Essential vs Signature)

One of the greatest pain points for homeowners in Chennai is opaque, shifting pricing. Many interior contractors quote low square-foot rates upfront, only to add substantial cost escalations for electrical modifications, hardware upgrades, and civil finishing.

At **Luxe Axis**, we operate with un-gated, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) where every single fastener, hinge, and square meter of laminate is priced transparently.

## 1. Cost per Square Foot vs Scope-Based Pricing

A flat "₹1,500/sq.ft" quote is often deceptive because it averages large empty floor areas with high-density joinery zones like kitchens and master wardrobes.

A realistic Chennai budget breakdown follows these tiers:
- **Essential Tier (₹1,800 – ₹2,200/sq.ft):** Calibrated HDHMR & BWP ply, anti-scratch matte laminates, German Hettich soft-close hardware, Saint-Gobain Gyproc false ceilings, and Philips LED lighting.
- **Signature Tier (₹2,600 – ₹3,200/sq.ft):** Italian PU high-gloss lacquer, anti-fingerprint acrylics, Blum Blumotion servo-drive hardware, fluted timber feature walls, and smart home lighting automation.
- **Elite Private Commission (₹4,000+/sq.ft):** Imported Italian Calacatta marble, bespoke teak joinery, architectural acoustic paneling, and custom luxury chandeliers.

## 2. Average Project Totals in Chennai

- **2BHK (800 – 1,100 sq.ft):** ₹7.5 Lakhs – ₹14 Lakhs
- **3BHK (1,300 – 1,800 sq.ft):** ₹12 Lakhs – ₹24 Lakhs
- **4BHK / Penthouse (2,200 – 3,500 sq.ft):** ₹22 Lakhs – ₹45 Lakhs
- **Independent Villa (3,000+ sq.ft):** ₹35 Lakhs – ₹80+ Lakhs
