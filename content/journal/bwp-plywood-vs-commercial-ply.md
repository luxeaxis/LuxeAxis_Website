# BWP Marine Plywood vs Commercial Ply: What Every Homeowner Must Know

Chennai’s coastal climate presents a unique engineering challenge for interior woodwork. With relative humidity regularly hovering between 70% and 90%, substandard plywood expands, absorbs ambient moisture, and delaminates within 24 to 36 months.

## 1. What is IS:710 Marine Grade Plywood?

To withstand coastal saline air and kitchen moisture, Luxe Axis exclusively specifies **IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Grade Plywood** for all wet areas:

- **Adhesive Standard:** Bonded with unextended Phenol Formaldehyde (PF) synthetic resin that does not dissolve even after 72 hours of continuous boiling water immersion.
- **Core Veneers:** 100% calibrated Gurjan / Eucalyptus hardwood plies with zero internal core gaps.
- **Termite & Borer Resistance:** Vacuum pressure impregnated with anti-pest biocides backed by a 10-year replacement warranty.

## 2. Why Commercial Ply (MR Grade) Fails in Kitchens

Commercial plywood (IS:303 MR — Moisture Resistant) uses Urea Formaldehyde resin. While suitable for dry bedroom wardrobes, when exposed to sink plumbing leaks, steam, or high coastal humidity:
1. The internal veneers separate, causing countertop sagging.
2. Screws in heavy hinge pockets lose tension, causing cabinet doors to drop out of alignment.
3. Fungal spores proliferate within internal voids, causing musty odors.
