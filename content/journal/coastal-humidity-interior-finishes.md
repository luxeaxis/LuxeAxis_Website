# Designing for Coastal Humidity: Anti-Rust Hardware & Mold-Resistant Finishes

Homes along ECR, Besant Nagar, Thiruvanmiyur, and Santhome face constant saline sea breeze and elevated atmospheric moisture. Standard nickel-plated hinges and porous MDF boards rapidly corrode, oxidize, and harbor mildew.

## 1. Anti-Corrosive Hardware Standards

For all residences within 10 km of the Bay of Bengal coastline, Luxe Axis mandates:
- **SS 304 / SS 316 Stainless Steel Hinges:** Multi-stage electro-plated and salt-spray tested to resist rust for over 200,000 opening cycles.
- **Concealed Magnetic Locks:** Eliminates mechanical spring friction prone to salt-crust jamming.
- **Hermetically Sealed LED Channels:** Silicone-gasketed aluminum extrusions prevent moisture ingress and driver burnout.

## 2. Mold-Resistant Wall & Cabinetry Finishes

- **Italian Anti-Bacterial PU Lacquer:** Non-porous polyurethane coating that seals timber cores against moisture penetration.
- **Anti-Fungal Emulsions:** Low-VOC, washable wall finishes treated with active silver-ion biocides to resist tropical mold bloom during northeast monsoon downpours.
