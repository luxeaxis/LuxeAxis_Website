# The 45-Day Handover Guarantee: How Factory Manufacturing Prevents Delays

Traditional carpenter-driven interior construction in India is notoriously prone to timeline creep. Dust, noise, manual cutting inaccuracies, and absenteeism frequently stretch 2-month projects into 6-month ordeals.

## 1. Parallel Factory Manufacturing vs Serial Site Work

At Luxe Axis, we separate manufacturing from site civil preparation:
1. **Days 1–15 (On-Site Civil & Electrical):** While false ceilings, electrical rewiring, and plumbing rough-ins occur on-site, modular cabinetry components are precision-cut via CNC machinery in our OMR manufacturing facility.
2. **Days 16–30 (Factory Edge-Banding & Assembly Check):** All carcases and shutters undergo German PUR hot-melt edge-banding and test dry-assembly in climate-controlled conditions.
3. **Days 31–40 (Modular Flatpack Installation):** Pre-drilled, calibrated cabinetry arrives flatpacked on-site and is installed using laser levelling in under 10 days.
4. **Days 41–45 (Snagging & Deep Cleaning):** Final hardware tensioning, lighting testing, professional deep cleaning, and client key handover.

## 2. Our Contractual Handover SLA

Every Luxe Axis project includes a contractually guaranteed completion date. If we exceed the committed handover milestone, we pay a daily compensation fee directly to the homeowner.
